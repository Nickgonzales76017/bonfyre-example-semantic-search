Agile methodology is an iterative approach to project management and software development that delivers work in small increments called sprints, typically lasting 1-4 weeks. The Agile Manifesto values individuals and interactions over processes, working software over documentation, customer collaboration over contracts, and responding to change over following a plan. Scrum is the most popular framework, with roles including Product Owner (defines priorities), Scrum Master (facilitates process), and Development Team. Key ceremonies are sprint planning, daily standups (15 minutes), sprint reviews, and retrospectives. Kanban is an alternative that visualizes workflow on a board with columns like To Do, In Progress, and Done, limiting work in progress to prevent bottlenecks. User stories follow the format "As a [role], I want [feature], so that [benefit]."
